## Briefing questions

Ask these before writing anything. Each answer changes the cut, the safe zone or the cost.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Platforms and format | 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts; 16:9 for a website or LinkedIn film; a silent loop without a visible seam for a sign-in page. Safe zones differ per platform. |
| Goal and call to action | Reach, sign-ups or explaining a feature decide the last beat and the link. Send cold traffic to a page that explains the product, not straight to a sign-up form. |
| Organic or paid | Paid placements go through ad review; keep a clean variant of any rough hook. |
| Languages | Each language gets its own script and timing table. |
| Voice and budget | A synthetic voice costs credits per character; a re-take costs the whole request again. |
| Length | Starting points: 20 to 32 s for vertical social (plus a 15 s cut), 45 to 75 s for LinkedIn, 60 to 80 s for a website film. |

## Worked example: an invoicing app, 30 seconds, vertical

The product sends payment reminders before an invoice is due. The fact check found the reminder schedule in
the product's settings page (3 days before the due date, then on the due date) and nothing about late-fee
handling, so the video does not mention late fees.

| Time (s) | Picture | Speech | Caption | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-2.5 | Frame 0: phone on a desk at night, lock screen with a client's mail "Why did nobody remind me?" | "Friday, 6 pm. A client is angry." | Friday, 6 pm. A client is angry. | phone buzz |
| 2.5-4.5 | second and third message stack on top, newest first | "Their invoice was due on Monday. Four days ago." | Their invoice was due on Monday. Four days ago. | buzz, low bed starts |
| 4.5-7.0 | mail opened: "We will pay late. And we are looking at other vendors." | "Now they are paying late and looking elsewhere." | Now they are paying late and looking elsewhere. | bed tightens |
| 7.0-8.0 | rewind cut, calendar runs back one week | "Same invoice, with reminders on." | Same invoice, with reminders on. | rewind |
| 8.0-14.0 | real product UI: reminder schedule, "3 days before due date" switched on; the reminder goes out on the Friday before | "Three days before it is due, the client gets a reminder." | Three days before it is due, the client gets a reminder. | soft chime |
| 14.0-19.0 | client's phone: the reminder mail arrives, "Pay now" | "One tap, and it is paid." | One tap, and it is paid. | chime |
| 19.0-25.0 | product dashboard: invoice turns from open to paid, timestamp Saturday | "Paid on Saturday. Nobody had to ask." | Paid on Saturday. Nobody had to ask. | bed resolves |
| 25.0-30.0 | lock screen on the due date, Monday morning, no messages; logo and call to action | "Get paid without chasing." | Get paid without chasing. | end tone |

Checks done on this example: the weekdays follow from one fixed due date (Monday; reminder the Friday before,
paid on the Saturday, the angry mail on the Friday after); "three days before" matches the setting. Rows longer
than 3 s (8 to 14, 19 to 25, 25 to 30) list one beat each and are split in the build into steps of 1.5 to 2.5 s:
a zoom, an arriving element or a caption change.
