# What experienced B2B marketers insist on

Use as a review list for any marketing plan. Most of it is practice, not a vendor rule; where a line has a
source it is named.

## Insist on

- Revenue outcomes: pipeline, sign-up to activation to paid, payback. Traffic, followers, page counts and
  "visibility scores" are inputs.
- Positioning before content: a named buyer, the alternative they use today, the moment the problem hurts.
  Without it every page and post reads generic.
- Original material: data from the product (aggregated, consented), screenshots, migration steps, real limits.
  Google asks for original information, research or analysis
  (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content, verified 2026-11-03).
- One product truth: prices, limits and features from config on every page, profile and deck.
- Honest comparisons: dated sources, the competitor's strengths, who should pick them.
- Third-party confirmation over own claims: complete directory profiles, real reviews, useful community
  answers, press built on data.
- Consent and opt-out handled before the first mail; German outreach rules before the first call.
- Patience with numbers: a channel or page is read after enough cohorts or weeks, against a control where
  possible (`search-measurement`).

## Agents typically get wrong

- Invented statistics, customer counts, logos, testimonials and case studies.
- Folklore stated as fact: answer lengths for AI, FAQ or HowTo schema as a lever, `llms.txt` as a citation
  driver (see `search-visibility`).
- US defaults: cold email sequences, buying lists, US review practice.
- Content at scale: a page per city or competitor pair without data of its own.
- Many channels at once, none measured to a paying customer.
- Success declared from one week of data or from upvotes.
- Copy inflated with superlatives and "10x" claims (`human-copy`).
- Editing the company's own Wikipedia article; Wikipedia's conflict-of-interest guideline strongly discourages
  it (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest, not re-read for this skill).
