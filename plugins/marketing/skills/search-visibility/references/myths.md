# Claims to refuse

Each claim appears in SEO tools, blogs or generated advice. Refuse it, cite the source, and offer what the
source supports instead. Read on 2026-11-03.

| Claim | What the source says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Schema is how AI Overviews or ChatGPT understand a page" | Structured data is not required for generative AI search; no special schema needed | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide |
| "Add FAQ schema for rich results" | FAQ rich results not shown since 7 May 2026 | https://developers.google.com/search/updates |
| "Add HowTo schema for step results" | HowTo rich results gone since 13 September 2023 | https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes |
| "llms.txt gets you cited by AI" | Google Search does not use it; no crawler page of OpenAI, Anthropic or Perplexity mentions it | https://developers.google.com/search/updates |
| "Pages need at least N words to rank" | Google has no preferred word count | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content |
| "AI answers need 40 to 60 word paragraphs" or "chunk content for AI" | No requirement to break content into pieces for AI | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide |
| "Rewrite pages specially for AI" | Not needed; write for visitors | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide |
| "IndexNow gets pages into Google faster" | Google is not an IndexNow participant | https://www.indexnow.org/searchengines.json |
| "Blocking GPTBot removes you from ChatGPT search" | Search uses `OAI-SearchBot`; `GPTBot` is training | https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots |
| "Blocking Google-Extended keeps you out of AI Overviews" | Google-Extended does not affect Google Search inclusion or ranking | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/google-common-crawlers |
| "Our Lighthouse score proves we pass Core Web Vitals" | Lab measurement is not a substitute for field measurement | https://web.dev/articles/vitals |
| "FID is a Core Web Vital" | INP is the successor metric to FID | https://web.dev/articles/inp |
| "Update the date so the page looks fresh" | Listed as a warning sign when content did not substantially change | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content |
| "Generate a page per city, industry or competitor pair" | Many pages without added value are scaled content abuse | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies |
| "Show star ratings for our product from our own reviews" | Self-serving reviews are ineligible; do not aggregate ratings from other sites | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet |
| "Tool X's AI visibility score measures ranking" | Third-party tools have no access to Google's ranking data | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/third-party-seo |

Not a myth, but unproven: specific answer lengths, citation-rate figures per schema type and weighted
"citability scores" have no vendor source. Say "no source" rather than repeat a number.
