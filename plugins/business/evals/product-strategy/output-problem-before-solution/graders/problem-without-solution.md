PASS if the Problem section of the brief says who has the problem, in which situation, what they do today (a workaround such as a spreadsheet, a group chat or paper) and what it costs them, and contains no product name, feature, screen or technology (app, dashboard, push notification, AI, scheduler). FAIL if the Problem section describes the product or a solution.
