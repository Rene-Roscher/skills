## How to re-verify

- German statutes: gesetze-im-internet.de, the section page plus the "Stand" line of the full text.
- EU acts: the EUR-Lex ELI link; check the "current consolidated version" and the list of amending acts.
- Authority practice (BSI, EDPB, Commission guidance): the authority's own page, with the date read.
- Write the date next to every citation you reuse. Anything not fetched stays Unverified.
