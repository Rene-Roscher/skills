# Terms: business metrics and company vocabulary

verified 2026-11-04. These are concepts, not products, so most rows have no official URL ("-"). Formulas differ between companies: confirm the project's definition before computing or comparing a number.

| term | aliases | what it is | not to confuse with | official URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MRR | monthly recurring revenue | Recurring revenue normalised to one month | cash received, revenue recognised | - |
| ARR | annual recurring revenue | Recurring revenue normalised to one year, often MRR times twelve | annual billing, bookings | - |
| ACV | annual contract value | Average yearly value of a contract | ARR (a total), TCV (total contract value) | - |
| CAC | customer acquisition cost | Sales and marketing cost to win one customer | CPA, CPL | - |
| LTV | CLV, customer lifetime value | Expected revenue or margin from a customer over their lifetime | MRR of one customer | - |
| NRR | net revenue retention, NDR | Revenue from a customer cohort after churn, downgrades and expansion | GRR | - |
| churn | Kündigungsquote, customer churn, revenue churn | Share of customers (or revenue) lost in a period | downgrade, pause | - |
| MoR | merchant of record, Verkäufer im Rechtssinne | Party that sells to the end customer, collects and remits the VAT and carries the payment liability | PSP, payment gateway | - |
| KMU | kleine und mittlere Unternehmen, SME, Mittelstand | Small and medium-sized enterprises | Kleinunternehmer (a VAT status) | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reco/2003/361/oj |
| DACH | - | Germany, Austria and Switzerland as a market region | the whole EU | - |
| SLA | Service Level Agreement | Contractual availability and support promise | SLO, SLI | - |
| SLO | Service Level Objective | Internal reliability target | SLA | https://sre.google/sre-book/service-level-objectives/ |
| netto/brutto | zzgl. MwSt., excl. VAT, incl. VAT | Price without or with VAT; B2B prices in Germany are usually quoted net | Rabatt, Skonto | - |
